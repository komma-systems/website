Mechanism design strategies

A mechanism is the working arrangement we build for a place: a concrete way for a community to hold land or housing in common. Most mechanisms draw on several of these strategies at once, in a mix shaped by the place.

A — hairline rows: domain, title, description (same system as the initiatives)
EconomicInverting Civic Wealth

When a place improves, the gains flow to whoever owns the property. We design ways to keep that value local and circulating: pooled funds, community-led exit strategies, multi-capital currencies, bioregional banking.

LegalModernising Agreements

Most of the unwinding happens in the legal form. We build modular agreements that communities can read, adapt and change: bundles of rights, distributed validation, customary claims given standing.

SocialCultivating Playful Rituals

Agreements hold when people live them. We fold governance into everyday life: sociocratic decision-making, ritual openings, and formats that connect the room with the record.

TechnologicalBuilding Civic Technology

Some mechanisms need software to run, so we build it: registries, pooled-commitment tools, deliberation infrastructure. It runs locally and stays in the hands of the people using it.
