One node among many

10x100 runs Braid and Silt as its own node. Other networks can run theirs, and strands can pass from one to another.

Komma and 10x100

An experiment in growing something together

Komma licenses Braid and Silt to 10x100 for this experiment and keeps the IP to develop with other networks. Members with projects beyond 10x100 can talk to Komma about running them on the same tools.
