MELD

Civic deliberation infrastructure · In deployment · Herzogtum Lauenburg + Ludwigslust-Parchim, DE · since 2026

Meld captures deliberation in the room, processes it on the device, and returns structured documentation to the people who spoke. Built with nextlearning, Max Planck Institute and the 10x100 network; deployed under the BMBF-funded InnoVER programme.

The problem

What communities say in a room rarely survives the room. Notes flatten disagreement, minutes arrive late, and sending audio to cloud AI trades trust for convenience. Placeholder: two more sentences on why deliberation needs its own infrastructure.

What Meld does

A hardware device and privacy-preserving platform. Consent is a physical NFC tap, audio never leaves the room, and the group receives its own sensemaking back as a queryable record. Placeholder: one sentence on BRAID / positions over time.

Deployments

First production deployment under InnoVER, a BMBF-funded public programme via the Landvorteil alliance, running pilots in two Landkreise. Demonstrated live with the 10x100 network at the European Forum Alpbach. Placeholder for deployment two.
