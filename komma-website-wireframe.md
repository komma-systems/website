## Rationale / decisions baked in

- **Above the fold = why + plain what + differentiator + 2 CTAs.** No tools, no jargon beyond "enclosures" (which the sub-line immediately translates).
- **Comma poem demoted** to a late "third floor" band — per the workshop, it lands once a reader knows what you do.
- **"Our Approach" → "How we work"**, recast as *read three registers → unlock*. Three equal cards; technology is a thin strip beneath, deliberately *not* a fourth pillar.
- **Threshold pools** gets its own band as living proof of the method — the highest-value single object on the page.
- **Initiatives foregrounded** (was buried low). Each card shows place · pain · phase, so a visitor sees where you are on each journey.
- **Team kept high**, against the usual "hide it under About" — because at this stage founders trust *people*, not the org.
- **Entry points** as a final band with doors per audience (municipalities / communities / funders / events).
